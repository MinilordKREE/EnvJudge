# M0 provenance — the `aea` scaffold

The scaffold is Project One's `ahd` M0 (MinilordKREE/agent_harnesses_diagnostic @
`db5807f1f0608184b141c80d37488e1c842d1d49`, the owner's own project; MIT), renamed to `aea`.
`ahd` itself adapted a few functions from public repositories; those chains are kept in the
module docstrings and in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

## Written fresh

`src/aea/__init__.py`, `src/aea/cli.py`, `src/aea/llm/envharness_client.py` (wraps the released
`LLMClient` ABC, see `docs/reuse/all.md`), `configs/pricing.yaml`, `tests/unit/*`, `LICENSE`.

## Not carried over

`ahd`'s response cache (`llm/cache.py`; no caches in experiments), the Evo-Bench task substrate,
harness snapshots, runner, diagnosis modules, and the environment probe.

## Pilots archived

`scratch/` (E-min, E-obs, E1-pilot P1–P5) moved to `docs/pilots/` at tag `m0`; nothing imports
from it at runtime. Their results, logs and pre-registrations stay tracked; their working
directories stay ignored.
