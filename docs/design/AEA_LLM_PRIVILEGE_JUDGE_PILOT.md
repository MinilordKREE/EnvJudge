## Question and known limitations

Can the existing iterative LOW implementation produce a useful admitted environment on
reused tasks 154/159 while using the current judge as an imperfect admission filter?
This is an engineering feasibility check, not a new judge benchmark, fresh task screening,
or a claim that semantic isolation or scientific efficacy has been established.

Round 5 remains JUDGE_GATE_NOT_READY: 11/13 core labels, 8/9 leaks FAIL and one UNCERTAIN,
3/4 legitimate controls PASS, 4/4 repeat agreement, and 19/21 final records without generated
uncertainty. All nine labeled leaks were blocked in this finite suite. The 349 independent
checks passed for evidence/request/accounting integrity; they do not prove semantic accuracy.
The known generic-prerequisite false rejection can consume DESIGN calls and limit which LOW
mechanisms are tried. Public-evidence scope errors also remain. No sixth prompt round is run.

1. Replay the four saved candidates in order 154 C1, 159 C1, 159 C2, 159 C3 through full admission.
   This stage performs no new DESIGN, solvability or policy evaluation. These inspections

   CONTROL. Keep endpoint reserve 16; a third proposal is not guaranteed a probe budget.
3. Preserve endpoint 4-to-8, too-hard redesign, first-viable-family freeze, too-easy CONTROL,

   controller decisions or acceptance feedback.

Stop on correctness/integrity failure, unresolved operational interruption, cap exhaustion,
or completion. No patch-and-resume, new tasks, D/I, E3, E3-SL, additional model comparisons,
or further judge/optimizer tuning during the pilot. Replay/adaptation stages cannot restart
implicitly; all physical retries remain visible and charged under the existing client.

## Interpretation and reporting

Keep the common driver's outcome precedence and report every task, including exhaustion,
rejections and unmeasured candidates. Report candidate verdict counts, endpoint measurements,
feedback lineage, freeze/CONTROL transitions, search acceptance, K16 and costs separately.
A lack of admitted candidates can leave LOW's usefulness unmeasured; it does not prove LOW
ineffective. Candidate verdicts alone cannot establish a false-positive rate on unlabeled
new proposals. Any JUDGE_GATE_BLOCKING conclusion requires the existing independent precision
evidence on both tasks, not an inferred label from a rejection or from poor performance.

A first-proposal success establishes pipeline functionality but does not by itself show
that iterative feedback improves proposals. Report actual C1-to-C2/C3 revisions separately.
A successful result supports end-to-end engineering viability under this particular gate
and reused-task protocol. It does not retroactively pass Phase A, validate the judge on new
data, establish information-theoretic protection, or authorize full AEA experiments.
