## Narrative (written after the tables; every number above comes from `scripts/make_tables_e6_assist.py`)

**Decision: RULE_GENERATION_FAILURE**, by the pre-registered rule applied in order: no gate failed, 8 tasks entered `zero` prospectively, 7 verified references (rule 1 not triggered); arm B has 0 K16-confirmed learnable deliveries (rule 2 not met); arm B ended in an upstream failure status (`no_valid_proposal`, `uncertified`, `no_leverage`, `dose_order_violation`) on 5 of the 7 referenced tasks (85, 99, 109 `no_valid_proposal`; 86, 97 `no_leverage`), more than half (rule 3). Rules 4 and 5 were not reached. Thresholds, bands and the classification are those of the prereg; nothing was changed after the results.

**What arm B (assistive Rules) did.** The designer produced syntactically loadable, dose-parameterised Rules on 4 of 7 tasks (7 valid families of 11 proposed). The three failures were the designer's own: a pydantic model constructed positionally (85), a family that embedded the task-specific reference action `go to bed 1` and the reference-only constant `pillow 1` and was rejected by the structural privilege check (99), and two families referring to an undefined name `DOSE` at module level (109). All 7 valid families passed the oracle guard at d = 1; 2 of 5 leverage-tested families had leverage at d = 1 (92: 4 of 8 in band, accepted directly; 107: 4 of 4 too easy). Task 107's family then bracketed inward and hit the 30-rollout cap with the bracket at [0.625, 0.75] after observing 0/4, 1/8 (too hard) and 4/4, 4/4 (too easy), i.e. the dose axis of this family behaved step-like between 0.625 and 0.75, exactly the behaviour the hypothesis assumed would not occur. Task 92's accepted environment scored 13 of 16 at K16 (p16 0.81), just above the learnable band B_L: a search accept that did not confirm. Arm B used 0 probe rollouts through the shared probe machinery (all its measurements are dose evaluations: 44 charged search rollouts beyond the replayed estimates over the 7 referenced tasks, against 136 for arm A; see the counts table) and USD 0.051 of designer spend.

**What arm A (stage control, frozen) did on the same evidence.** As in phase 3.3b, t_max was too easy on all 7 referenced tasks, every task had leverage, and the integer bracket ended `resolution_limited` on 3 tasks (gaps [2, 3], [4, 5], [1, 2]) and at the budget on 3 tasks; the one search accept (85, t = 10, 3 of 8) scored 3 of 16 at K16 (p16 0.19), just below B_L. Arm A had an intermediate probe (0 < successes < n) on 6 tasks, arm B on 3.

**Reading.** On this pool the LLM-generated assistive families were not a finer control axis than Stage depth: they mostly had no leverage at all (the policy ignored observation-side reminders), and the one family that had graded leverage showed the same step-like response along its dose that Stage depth shows along t. Both arms produced exactly one search accept, and both accepts failed K16 in opposite directions (0.19 and 0.81), consistent with the earlier finding that the 8-rollout accept criterion is noisy at the band edges. The paired ordinal comparison favours A on 4 tasks, B on 1 (92), with 3 ties. The generation failures are a limitation of the one-call, no-repair protocol that was pre-registered on purpose; they are recorded, not patched.

**Audit-tool corrections (logged; pre-correction outputs archived under `e6_low_assistive_rules/pre_correction/`).** The chain's first tables reported INCONCLUSIVE because (1) the arm B mode gate read a field that only the refalign-era proposal event fills, whereas this variant records `mode = assist` in its designer record (7 of 7 present); (2) the confirm stage collected only Setup-prefix accepts and skipped arm B's accepted Rules candidate, which the prereg requires to be confirmed (it was confirmed once at K = 16 after the fix, under the default reset its search probes used); (3) the leverage / resolution metrics ignored the d = 1 measurement recorded only in the `no_leverage` event or in an in-band accept at d = 1 (task 97's 1 of 8 and task 92's 4 of 8); (4) the rule-3 count added class counts to reason counts and exceeded the number of referenced tasks; it now counts tasks once by their final status. None of the corrections changed the method, the prereg, the thresholds or the decision (rule 3 holds with 5 of 7, and also with 4 of 7 if task 97's 1 of 8 were read as leverage).

**Interruption.** The machine was restarted during the shared stage (LOG 2026-09-14 ~18:10Z / 20:14Z). Seven shared estimates were on disk; task 109's interrupted estimate (8 rollouts, USD 1.08) was set aside (`traces.interrupted-109.jsonl`, never replayed) and re-run from scratch; its cost is inside the shared total.

**Relation to EnvHarness-style refinement (conceptual, no superiority claim).** EnvHarness-style loops let the LLM diagnose, generate or modify the environment program, observe fresh rollouts and refine the program again, i.e. they search over programs. The phase-3.4 hypothesis was that one LLM diagnosis can emit a parameterised assistive family W(d) and that current-policy feedback then searches over operating points d inside that family. This experiment says nothing about refinement loops; it says that, without repair, the one-shot family generation was the dominant failure on this pool (3 of 7 tasks produced no loadable or non-privileged family, 2 more had no leverage at d = 1), and that where a family had graded leverage its dose response was step-like, so the operating-point search inherited the same resolution limit as Stage depth.

**Scope.** Phase 3.4 ends here: no full E6, no E6-SL, no further LOW mechanism, no repair or refinement round follows from this result.
