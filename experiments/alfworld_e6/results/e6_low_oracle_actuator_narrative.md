## Narrative (written after the tables; every number above comes from `scripts/make_tables_e6_oracle.py`)

**Decision: ASSISTIVE_RULES_ACTUATOR_NOT_SUPPORTED**, by the pre-registered rule applied in order: all gates pass (rule 1 not triggered); a family was available and valid on 7 of 7 tasks (rule 2 not triggered); d = 1 leverage on 3 of 7 tasks, below the 5 of 7 that rules 3 and 4 require; rule 5 applies. One task (99) produced a K16-confirmed learnable environment (6 of 16, p16 0.38 in B_L), against 0 of 7 for the phase-3.4 generated families; that is reported descriptively and does not change the state. This is an oracle ceiling: the families were hand-verified privileged assistance and the numbers are not AEA, llm_v1 or LOW performance.

**What happened per class.** Class S, explored-receptacle pruning (85, 109): at d = 1 every already-inspected, goal-free receptacle was removed from the navigation choices and the policy still scored 0 of 4 on both tasks: the current policy does not convert a shorter choice list into reaching the untried surface (it keeps examining, looking or re-opening what is in front of it). Class G, goal-decomposition annotation (86): the sub-step line on every observation gave 0 of 4; the policy that sees `plate 1` and searches cabinets did not act on "find a plate and take it". Class P, precondition gating (92, 97, 99, 107): blocking the premature completing action with the goal precondition had leverage on 3 of 4 tasks. On 92 (cellphone on the armchair, 48-step reference search) it had none: blocked from using the desklamp, the policy still did not search. On 97 the gate made the task too easy at every dose down to 0.125 (blocking only one premature placement in eight was enough for 4 of 4) and dead at 0.0625: a knife-edge between 0.0625 and 0.125, bracket exhausted (`exhausted` after the four pre-registered bisections; 30 rollouts including the replayed 10). On 107 the opposite: 4 of 4 at d = 1 and 0 of 4 at 0.5, 0.75, 0.875 and 0.9375, i.e. only the fully closed gate works, another step between 0.9375 and 1. On 99 the bracket landed at d = 0.5 (5 of 8) after 4 of 4 at d = 1, and K16 confirmed it at 6 of 16.

**Reading for the decomposition question.** Removing generation reliability did not make the LOW dose controller viable on this pool. Where the hand-verified family had no leverage (4 of 7), the generated family of phase 3.4 also had none or did not exist, except 92, where the generated observation-side family (hide the lamp action + search reminder) had 4 of 8 at d = 1 and the hand-verified action-side gate had 0 of 4: two mechanisms addressing the same diagnosis differ in leverage, so mechanism choice, not only code realisation, matters. Where the oracle family had leverage (3 of 7), the coverage dose behaved step-like on 2 of 3 (97, 107) and the bracket could not land within the matched budget; the one landing (99) was a genuine finite-sample accept that confirmed. The behavioural resolution of the dose axis was one intermediate regime on 1 of 7 tasks (99: 5 of 8), against 3 of 7 for the generated families in phase 3.4 and 6 of 7 for Stage depth (which has leverage everywhere because the prefix does the work). The ordering that emerges over three LOW mechanisms is: leverage is the scarce resource (Stage 7/7, generated 3/7, oracle 3/7), and once a mechanism has leverage the 4 -> 8 acceptance with a 30-rollout cap lands about once per pool regardless of the axis (Stage 1, generated 1, oracle 1).

**Descriptive comparison with phase 3.4 (no rerun).** Generated: 0 of 7 K16-confirmed, 3 of 7 with leverage, 3 no valid proposal. Oracle: 1 of 7 K16-confirmed, 3 of 7 with leverage, 7 of 7 valid. The hand-verified families closed the validity gap entirely (no code error, no privilege rejection) and improved the primary count by one task; they did not raise leverage.

**Design restriction (recorded).** The families were designed from the goal, the failed trajectories, the reference and the Rules contract under one rubric and one coverage-dose semantics fixed before any task; no phase-3.4 response measurement selected a mechanism or a dose; no current-policy call was made before the freeze (23e87aa); no family was changed after it.
